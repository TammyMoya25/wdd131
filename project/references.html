Project References and Assets Citations

This page documents the real sources, data, and media used to build this website project for academic purposes (WDD 131).

  • Cost of Living and Budget Data: Sourced from Nomad List - Dominican Republic.
  • Destination Guides and Local Attractions: Information about Santo Domingo, Las Terrenas, and Cabarete gathered from the Go Dominican Republic Official Tourism Website.
  • Internet Speed and Coworking Information: Data compiled from active digital nomad community forums on Reddit r/digitalnomad.
  • Typography: Poppins and Open Sans font families sourced from Google Fonts.
  • Visual Assets and Photography: All optimized images curated from high-quality, open-license creators on Unsplash.
