Digital Nomad Life in the Dominican Republic

Site Name

🌴💻 Digital Nomad Life in the Dominican Republic

This name clearly describes the website's purpose and target audience. It focuses on people interested in spending one month living and working remotely in the Dominican Republic while enjoying its tropical lifestyle, beaches, culture, and affordability.

Site Purpose

This website will serve as a travel and lifestyle guide for remote workers who want to spend one month in the Dominican Republic. Visitors will find destination recommendations, accommodation suggestions, estimated monthly budgets, transportation information, internet and coworking details, local attractions, and travel planning resources.

The website will also include interactive tools such as destination recommendations, favorite locations, and a travel checklist using JavaScript.

Scenarios

  • What is the best city in the Dominican Republic for remote work and reliable internet?
  • How much money should I budget for one month as a digital nomad?
  • Which locations offer the best combination of beaches, safety, and coworking spaces?
  • What should I pack before spending one month in the Dominican Republic?
